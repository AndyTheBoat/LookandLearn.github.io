Privacy notice

This notice describes this public static reviewer demo only. It is for adult reviewers and parents using fictional data. It is not a production child service or the privacy policy for the separate local Django application.

Project and contact

The demonstration is maintained through the GitHub account AndyTheBoat for the Look and Learn project. Contact the maintainer through repository issues. Issues are public: never post names of children, private answers, credentials or other sensitive information. Legal operator details and a private support channel must be established before a production launch.

What this page processes

The demo processes your selected question set, video link, typed sample answers and session counts in browser memory. It does not send these answers or scores to a Look and Learn server, persist them in cookies or browser storage, request a microphone, or create accounts. Reloading or closing the page clears the application’s temporary practice state. The Clear session progress button clears question progress while the current page is open. Browser history, developer tools and third-party services can retain their own information.

GitHub hosting

Pages and images are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information including visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its own retention and user rights are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. Look and Learn does not add analytics, advertising trackers or a tracking cookie.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services, specifically the IFrame Player API. Until you select the adult-review consent checkbox and submit a valid video link, this page does not load the YouTube player or its API script. Loading it sends requests, the selected video identifier and technical information such as your IP address and site origin to YouTube/Google. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embedding domain; this does not mean that Google processes no data or that playing a video cannot involve storage or identifiers. YouTube may show advertisements and links.

Read the Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube embedding guidance. YouTube data handling is controlled by Google. Use your browser’s privacy controls and Google’s privacy tools for its data; clearing our session counts does not erase third-party records.

Children and future deployment

Do not enter real child information in this demonstration. The intended future product concerns children and parental learning choices, but this adult-review demo does not collect child profiles. Target ages, countries, parental consent, child-directed API notification, legal operator information and production retention/deletion controls remain launch requirements. No completed child-safety or legal compliance audit is claimed.

Storage, sharing and deletion

We do not maintain a database of demo answers or scores, so there is no Look and Learn stored session record to export or delete. We do not sell or share those answers. Hosting and player providers independently receive the technical data described above. Contact the maintainer about this notice without including sensitive information in a public issue.
