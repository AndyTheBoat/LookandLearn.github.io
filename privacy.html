Privacy policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Public demonstration version

This policy covers the Stop and Learn demonstration at andytheboat.github.io/LookandLearn.github.io/. Please read and agree to this policy before using the demo. It explains actual current practices; planned production features are not treated as existing features.

1. Who operates this demonstration

Stop and Learn is an individually developed, privately funded proof of concept maintained by the GitHub account AndyTheBoat. There are no real customers yet. For questions or complaints about privacy, contact the project maintainer through the repository contact channel. This channel is public: describe the issue without posting personal information, children’s information, account credentials or private answers. A private contact channel and the operator’s full legal identity must be established before a production launch.

2. What information the demo processes and why

InformationPurpose and handling
Privacy agreementHeld in browser memory to enable the demo for this page session. No stored consent log or identifier.
Selected question set and pause intervalUsed in browser memory to deliver the sample practice flow.
YouTube link or video IDUsed to load the video you select in YouTube’s player. The video ID is sent to YouTube when you load it.
Typed sample answers, question progress and correct-answer countUsed in browser memory for independent practice feedback and temporary counts. These answers and counts are not sent by our application code to a Stop and Learn server, GitHub or YouTube.
Player state and playback positionRead through the IFrame Player API in browser memory to time practice breaks. Not saved as a viewing history.
Technical request informationGitHub and YouTube/Google receive technical information when your browser connects to their services, as explained below.

Do not enter names, real child data or sensitive information. Questions are independent of video content. This demo does not request a microphone, upload recordings, analyse videos, create child profiles, collect payment details or offer accounts. It does not use the YouTube Data API search endpoint or collect search queries. Future search and stored learning history require an updated policy and appropriate consent before introduction.

3. YouTube API Services and Google

Stop and Learn uses YouTube API Services, specifically the YouTube IFrame Player API. We load the player API and embed only after you agree to this policy and submit a valid video link. YouTube supplies video content, player controls, related content and, where applicable, advertisements. YouTube/Google can process the selected video identifier, IP address, browser/device information, site origin/referrer and interactions with their player under the Google Privacy Policy.

The embed uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. This is not a promise of anonymous viewing or zero data collection. Third-party player content may store, access or recognise information on your browser or device, including cookies, local storage, identifiers or similar technologies, in accordance with the provider’s settings and policies. Playing a video can involve additional processing. Your Google sign-in state and browser privacy settings can affect the provider’s behaviour. See YouTube’s embedding guidance.

Use of the YouTube functionality is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, also linked in our demonstration terms. We do not download, cache or store video/audio copies. We do not sell advertising or alter advertisements within YouTube content.

4. Google account access and revocation

The demo does not ask you to sign in with Google, request OAuth permissions, access private YouTube account information or store Google access tokens. Never provide your Google or YouTube password to Stop and Learn. Signing in within a provider’s own interface is governed by that provider.

You can inspect and revoke permissions you have granted to applications through Google account security permissions. This demo currently requests no such authorised access, so a Stop and Learn entry is not expected. This link does not delete Google’s own player or hosting records. Any future authorised-account feature must first implement consent, revocation and the applicable deletion requirements, with an updated privacy policy.

5. Hosting, sharing and device storage

GitHub Pages serves this website, its scripts and logo. GitHub receives technical requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its handling is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting external links connects you to those providers under their policies.

Stop and Learn’s demo code does not add analytics, tracking pixels, application cookies or persistent browser storage. We do not maintain a server database of demo answers, sell them, share them with advertisers, or use them to train models. GitHub and YouTube independently process their technical and service data; we cannot promise deletion of their records or make claims about their retention periods. Services may process data in countries different from yours under their own policies.

6. Retention, clearing data and withdrawing agreement

Our application state lasts only while the page is open. Clear session progress removes temporary answer text, feedback, question progress and practice counts; it leaves the selected video and settings available. Withdraw agreement and clear demo reloads the page, clears its application state and removes the embedded player from the page. The reloaded page makes no YouTube player requests until you agree again and submit a video. Closing or reloading the page also ends this temporary state.

These controls do not clear your browser history, provider cookies, provider logs, backups managed by those providers or any information you voluntarily post in a public GitHub issue. Manage browser storage through browser settings; use Google and GitHub privacy tools for their data and contact the maintainer for concerns about information posted in this repository. No fixed provider deletion timeline is represented by this policy.

7. Children and the scope of this review

This public proof of concept is for adult reviewers and parents using fictional sample data. It is not offered as a production service for children. The intended future product will let children choose permitted videos and parents choose independent learning questions. Before child-facing deployment, the operator must define target ages and countries, appropriate parental consent and supervision, child-directed API notification, private support, and retention/deletion and security controls. This policy does not assert that those future launch requirements have already been completed.

8. Your choices, security and changes

You may decline agreement and read the public information pages without loading YouTube. You can withdraw as described above, avoid entering sensitive information, and use browser and provider privacy controls. The published site is served over HTTPS, but no website or third-party service can guarantee absolute security. Privacy rights depend on your location and the relevant provider; the links above explain provider-specific requests.

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. The demo requires agreement on each fresh page load and does not remember acceptance. New data uses must be disclosed before they begin. Questions or complaints can be raised through the maintainer’s public contact channel, without disclosing private information.
