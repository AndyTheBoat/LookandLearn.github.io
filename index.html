Their video choice.
Your learning goals.

Children have their own interests. Stop and Learn preserves video choice and gives parents a way to add independent question-and-answer practice to viewing time. A video about any subject can accompany maths or vocabulary chosen by the parent.

The proposed benefit for YouTube is greater parental confidence in viewing, continued creator discovery, and learning practice around playback through YouTube’s own player. These benefits are hypotheses to test.
